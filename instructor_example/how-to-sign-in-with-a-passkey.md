# How to Sign In with a Passkey

## Summary

Sign in to SecurePath using a passkey to authenticate with your device's fingerprint sensor, face recognition, or PIN — without entering a code from your authenticator app.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A passkey already set up on this device (see [How to set up a passkey](how-to-set-up-a-passkey.md))
- A supported browser on the device where the passkey is stored

## Steps

1. Go to the SecurePath sign-in page and enter your email address and password.

2. Click **Sign in**.

   Result: SecurePath verifies your password and displays the passkey prompt.

   <!-- IMAGE: screenshot of the passkey prompt screen, showing the "Use your passkey" message and the device biometric icon -->

3. Follow your device's prompt to confirm your identity. Depending on your device, you will be asked to use your fingerprint, face, or PIN.

   Result: Your device signs the authentication request. SecurePath verifies the response and signs you in.

## Result

You are signed in to SecurePath. The sign-in typically completes within a few seconds of your biometric confirmation.

---

## If your passkey device is not available

If you are signing in from a device that does not have your passkey, or your passkey is unavailable, you can use a different sign-in method:

1. On the passkey prompt screen, click **Use a different method**.

   <!-- IMAGE: screenshot of the passkey prompt with the "Use a different method" link highlighted -->

2. Select one of the available alternatives:
   - **Authenticator app** — enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app
   - **Backup code** — enter one of your saved backup codes
