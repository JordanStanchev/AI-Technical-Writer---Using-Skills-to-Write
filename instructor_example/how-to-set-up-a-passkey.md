# How to Set Up a Passkey

## Summary

Set up a passkey to replace your authenticator app code with a faster, more secure sign-in using your device's fingerprint sensor, face recognition, or PIN.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- An active SecurePath account
- A device with a fingerprint sensor, face recognition, or PIN set up (for example: a MacBook with Touch ID, an iPhone, a Windows PC with Windows Hello, or a physical security key such as a YubiKey)
- A supported browser (Chrome 108+, Safari 16.1+, Firefox 119+, or Edge 108+)

## Steps

1. Sign in to SecurePath with your email, password, and current two-factor authentication method.

2. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner of the page.

   Result: The account menu opens.

3. Select **Settings**.

   Result: The Settings page opens.

4. Select **Security** from the left navigation.

   <!-- IMAGE: screenshot of the Settings page with Security selected in the left navigation -->

5. Under **Two-Factor Authentication**, click **Add Passkey**.

   Result: The Add Passkey dialog opens.

   <!-- IMAGE: screenshot of the Two-Factor Authentication section with the Add Passkey button visible -->

6. Follow your device's prompt to confirm your identity. Depending on your device, you will be asked to use your fingerprint, face, or PIN.

   Result: Your device creates the passkey. The Add Passkey dialog confirms that the passkey was set up successfully.

   <!-- IMAGE: screenshot of the device biometric prompt (use a macOS Touch ID example) -->

7. Optional: Enter a name for the passkey in the **Passkey name** field — for example, "Work MacBook" or "YubiKey". This helps you identify the passkey if you set up more than one.

8. Click **Save**.

## Result

Your passkey is saved. The next time you sign in to SecurePath, you will be prompted to use your passkey instead of entering a code from your authenticator app.

To verify the passkey was saved, go to **Settings** > **Security** > **Two-Factor Authentication**. Your new passkey appears in the **Passkeys** list.
